# FastDVDnet weights

`model.pth` and `model_clipped_noise.pth` are the pretrained FastDVDnet weights from
<https://github.com/m-tassano/fastdvdnet> (commit c8fdf6182a0340e89dd18f5df25b47337cbede6f),
by Matias Tassano, Julie Delon and Thomas Veit, *FastDVDnet: Towards Real-Time Deep Video
Denoising Without Flow Estimation*, CVPR 2020. Redistributed unchanged under the MIT licence
in `LICENSE`.
